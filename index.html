Social Uploader

Social Uploader is a small personal automation tool. Its owner uses it to upload and schedule his or her own videos on the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, and to move video files from a folder in the owner's own Google Drive to the computer that runs the tool. The owner controls everything from a private Telegram chat.

The tool is not offered to the public and has a single user: its owner. It does not collect or sell anyone's data.

The source code is a private project of Hitesh-Patil01.
